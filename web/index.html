Get the test version

Moly is in closed testing on Google Play. Three steps, on your Android phone. Use the Google account your Play Store is signed in with for all three.

  1. Join the testers group. Open appblock-testers and tap Join group.
  2. Become a tester. Open the testing page and tap Become a tester. It then says You are a tester.
    Google Play may take about 30 minutes to catch up. Until then, Moly's Play Store page may say Item not found. If you did steps 1 and 2 and saw You are a tester., you're all set: go on to step 3, and if Play isn't ready yet, try again a little later.
  3. Install Moly. Open Moly on Google Play and tap Install. If Play still says Item not found., give it a little longer and try again.
Know someone who'd like to try it? Share this page, this page, not the Play Store or group links. Those only work after the steps above, in order.
