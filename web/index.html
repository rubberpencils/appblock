Get the test version

AppBlock is in closed testing on Google Play. Three steps, on your Android phone. Use the Google account your Play Store is signed in with for all three.

  1. Join the testers group. Open appblock-testers and tap Join group.
  2. Become a tester. Open the testing page and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install AppBlock. Open AppBlock on Google Play and tap Install. If Play says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes and try again.
Know someone who'd like to try it? Share this page, this page, not the Play Store or group links. Those only work after the steps above, in order.
