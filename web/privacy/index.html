Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

AppBlock doesn't collect your data or send it anywhere. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

To block apps, AppBlock sees which apps are on your screen and, if you allow it, how long you use them. That stays on your phone. It never reads your messages, what you type or your passwords.

If you allow usage access, AppBlock also keeps a history of which apps you used and when, so it can show your screen time day by day. It stays on your phone: it isn't sent anywhere and is left out of Android's backups.

If you turn on strict mode, AppBlock also looks at the screens of Settings, the uninstall prompt and the permission pages for its own name, so it can cover the ones that would switch it off. It looks for that one word and keeps nothing it sees there. Strict mode also makes AppBlock a device admin, only so that it can't be uninstalled while strict mode is on: it gets no other admin powers, and lets go when strict mode ends. Strict mode's settings are left out of Android's backups.

Uninstalling AppBlock deletes everything it stored.
