Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Moly doesn't collect your data or send it anywhere, unless you choose to write to us from the app. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

To block apps, Moly sees which apps are on your screen and, if you allow it, how long you use them. That stays on your phone. It never reads your messages, what you type or your passwords.

If you allow usage access, Moly also keeps a history of which apps you used and when, so it can show your screen time day by day. It stays on your phone: it isn't sent anywhere and is left out of Android's backups.

If you turn on strict mode, Moly also looks at the screens of Settings, the uninstall prompt and the permission pages for its own name, so it can cover the ones that would switch it off. It looks for that one word and keeps nothing it sees there. Strict mode also makes Moly a device admin, only so that it can't be uninstalled while strict mode is on: it gets no other admin powers, and lets go when strict mode ends. Strict mode's settings are left out of Android's backups.

If you block Shorts or Reels, Moly downloads updated detection rules from trymoly.com every few hours while that app is open. The download sends nothing about you or your phone. Like any download, it shows the server your IP address; we keep no record of it.

If you send feedback or a support request from the app, we receive what you wrote, your email address if you gave one (a support request needs one, so we can answer), and a few details that help us understand it: Moly's version, your phone's model, Android version and language, when Moly was installed and last updated, and how Moly is set up: which of its permissions are on, whether strict mode and Shorts or Reels blocking are on, how many apps you limit, and the date of its detection rules. With it goes a random ID that Moly made up, so we can tell which messages come from the same person; it says nothing about you or your phone. We also see the country it was sent from. We never receive which apps you set limits on, or how you use any app. What you send is stored on our server, which Cloudflare hosts, and emailed to the developer. As with any request, the server's log may keep your IP address for a few days; it is not stored with your message. A copy of what you sent stays on your phone and in its backup. To have what you sent deleted, write to the address below.

Uninstalling Moly deletes everything it stored on your phone.

This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you.
