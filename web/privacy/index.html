Privacy policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Moly sends nothing about you anywhere, unless you write to us from the app. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

On your phone

  • Blocking. Moly sees which apps are on your screen and, if you allow it, how long you use them. It never reads your messages, what you type or your passwords.
  • Screen time (usage access, optional). A history of which apps you used and when. Left out of Android's backups.
  • Strict mode (optional). Moly looks for its own name on Settings, uninstall and permission screens, to cover the ones that would switch it off, and keeps nothing it sees. It becomes a device admin only so it can't be uninstalled, with no other admin powers. Left out of Android's backups.

All of this stays on your phone. Uninstalling Moly deletes it.

What Moly downloads

If you block Shorts or Reels, Moly fetches detection rules from trymoly.com every few hours while that app is open. It sends nothing about you. As with any download, the server sees your IP address; we keep no record of it.

If you write to us

Feedback or a support request sent from the app gives us:

  • what you wrote, and your email address if you gave one;
  • Moly's version, your phone's model, Android version and language, and when Moly was installed and last updated;
  • how Moly is set up: which permissions are on, whether strict mode and Shorts or Reels blocking are on, how many apps you limit, and the date of its detection rules;
  • a random ID Moly made up, so we can tell which messages come from the same person;
  • the country it was sent from.

Never which apps you limit, or how you use any app. It is stored on our server, hosted by Cloudflare, and emailed to the developer. The server's log may keep your IP address for a few days, apart from your message. A copy stays on your phone and in its backup.

Deleting what you sent

Moly, by rubberpencils, deletes your messages when you ask. Ask from the app, on the phone you sent them from: the request carries the random ID, which is how we find them.

  1. Open Moly and tap the i in the circle.
  2. Tap Help & feedback and set it to a support request.
  3. Ask for your messages to be deleted, add your email, and tap Send.

We delete every message sent with that ID and everything that came with it, from our server and the developer's mailbox, and keep nothing. Until you ask, messages are kept. No longer have Moly? Write to the address below from the email you gave.
