Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Moly doesn't collect your data or send it anywhere. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

To block apps, Moly sees which apps are on your screen and, if you allow it, how long you use them. That stays on your phone. It never reads your messages, what you type or your passwords.

If you allow usage access, Moly also keeps a history of which apps you used and when, so it can show your screen time day by day. It stays on your phone: it isn't sent anywhere and is left out of Android's backups.

If you turn on strict mode, Moly also looks at the screens of Settings, the uninstall prompt and the permission pages for its own name, so it can cover the ones that would switch it off. It looks for that one word and keeps nothing it sees there. Strict mode also makes Moly a device admin, only so that it can't be uninstalled while strict mode is on: it gets no other admin powers, and lets go when strict mode ends. Strict mode's settings are left out of Android's backups.

If you block Shorts or Reels, Moly downloads updated detection rules from trymoly.com every few hours while that app is open. The download sends nothing about you or your phone. Like any download, it shows the server your IP address; we keep no record of it.

Uninstalling Moly deletes everything it stored.

This website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you.
